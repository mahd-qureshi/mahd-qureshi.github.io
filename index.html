A little background

I grew up in Aramco compounds, curious about the people and systems around me. I built Moji partly because I wanted a companion I could turn to whenever a question came up.

Further ahead, I’m interested in communication across species, robotics, new kinds of sport, and better ways to travel. These are long-term directions; LifeHub is my focus today.

Outside the build: chess, skateboarding, and audio engineering.
