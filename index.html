Projects / Open source

Continuity Engine.

Interactive storytelling

Make a decision.
Change how the story unfolds.

I’m exploring stories you can influence. Continuity Engine is an open-source prototype that remembers choices, tracks character state, and proposes what could happen next.

The long-term idea: a TV-style experience where your decisions shape how a series unfolds. This engine is an early step toward that.

Try the demo ↗Read the source ↗
Under the hood +

Choices become immutable events; character state is rebuilt by replaying them. A constrained beam search proposes a three-beat narrative path, with an inspectable decision trace.

JavaScript · v0.2 · Zero dependencies · Zero API calls

I called home.

Curiosity about the whole.
Attention to the details.

I grew up in Aramco compounds. I remapped the entire compound I called home to understand how its layout, energy systems, and place in the surrounding ecosystem fit together.

I’ve always been curious about the places and people around me. That same curiosity led me to build Moji: a companion I could turn to whenever a question came up.
