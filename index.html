For the student who needs to hear: we can work through this.

When a whole semester feels like too much, the next step can feel impossible. LifeHub is my response: a digital companion designed to help students break things down and work through what comes next.

At its center is Moji: one character, in a single-screen experience. A companion for your questions, your ideas, and the day in front of you. I built every part of LifeHub, from engineering to design.

03 / Taste

Make it feel right.

My instinct for LifeHub was to keep the experience centered on one screen and one character. I want it to feel like a muse you can turn to. That’s where I put my taste to work: in the decisions that shape the experience.

03 / Another side of the workOpen source
Interactive storytelling · JavaScriptContinuity Engine+

A small, deterministic engine for interactive stories that remembers choices, updates emotional state, and explains what should happen next.

I wanted character continuity that could be replayed and inspected. Choices become immutable events; state is rebuilt by replaying them. A constrained beam search proposes a three-beat narrative path, with a decision trace you can inspect.

v0.2 · Zero dependencies · Zero API calls
